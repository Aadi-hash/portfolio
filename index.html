Profile

I'm Aaditya, a software developer who likes problems with a hard edge. I'd rather understand how a scheduler picks its next thread, or why a model fits in a phone's memory, than glue boxes together.

General notes

  1. Go one layer deeper

    Abstractions are great until they leak. I like knowing what's underneath: the kernel, the runtime, the maths.

  2. Ship the small version

    A working slice beats a perfect plan. Build it, measure it, then earn the next piece of complexity.

  3. Make it feel good

    Performance and polish are features. If it stutters or confuses, it isn't finished yet.

On-device AI · Android

Waste Segregation Assistant

Point a phone at trash; it identifies the item, picks the right bin under India's waste rules and explains why. Vision and an LLM run fully offline.

  • ExecuTorch
  • Llama
  • Arm
  • Python
YEAR
2026
RUNS ON
Android · Arm
STATUS
In progress

Sorting waste correctly is confusing, and the right answer depends on the item. The assistant looks at something through the phone camera and says which bin it belongs in under India's waste rules, with a short explanation.

A vision model identifies the item and a small Llama model explains the disposal and recycling options. Both run on the phone through ExecuTorch, so nothing leaves the device and it works with no network.

Early. The repository, tooling and design are in place; the model work comes next.

Computer vision · Sport

Tennis Serve Analyzer

Personalised serve biomechanics from video: track the body through the motion and compare it against professional serves to show what to change.

  • Pose estimation
  • Biomechanics
  • Python
YEAR
2026
INPUT
Serve video
STATUS
In design

A serve is one fast, complicated motion, and generic tips rarely fit a specific player. The goal is feedback personalised to one player's body and technique.

Estimate the body's pose through the serve from video, turn that into biomechanical measures, and compare them with professional serves to point at specific differences.

In design, with an early implementation. The architecture and pipeline are chosen.

Pintos Kernel

  • GDB

Pintos is a small teaching operating system. The work is to extend it from the inside until it behaves like a real kernel.

Work through threads and scheduling, synchronisation and inter-process communication, running the kernel in an emulator inside Docker and debugging with GDB.

Coursework, in progress this academic year.

Quantum computing

Quantum State Lab

Pure and mixed states, state vectors and density matrices explored in simulation, with notebooks that show the maths and the measurements side by side.

  • Qiskit
  • Qiskit Aer
  • Jupyter
YEAR
2026
BACKEND
Aer simulator
STATUS
Coursework

A team assignment on how quantum states are described: pure and mixed states, state vectors and density matrices.

Build each case in Qiskit, run it on the Aer simulator, and put the mathematics next to the measured results in notebooks so the two can be checked against each other.

Coursework. Most sections are implemented and the rest are scaffolded.
